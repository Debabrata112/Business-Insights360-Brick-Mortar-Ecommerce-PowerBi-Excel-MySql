# Business-Insights360-Brick-Mortar-Ecommerce-PowerBi-Excel-MySql
## Problem Statement

The company was facing challenges in managing and analyzing business data due to reliance on **legacy Excel reports** and data being distributed across multiple sources, including Excel/CSV files and SQL databases. This made it difficult for management to obtain a centralized view of business performance and efficiently monitor key metrics across different departments.

The business needed a scalable reporting solution to analyze **revenue, expenses, sales, profitability, and departmental performance** while enabling management to identify trends, monitor targets, and make data-driven decisions.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project was to develop a **centralized Power BI Business Intelligence solution** that integrates data from multiple sources and provides management with an interactive view of overall business performance.

The project focused on:

* Consolidating data from **Excel/CSV files and MySQL databases** into a unified reporting model.
* Replacing manual and legacy **Excel-based reporting** with an interactive Power BI solution.
* Developing dashboards to monitor **revenue, expenses, sales, and departmental performance**.
* Creating optimized **DAX measures and data models** for efficient reporting.
* Analyzing revenue and expense trends to identify areas for improvement.
* Enabling management to monitor business targets and support strategic decisions.
## Key Achievements

* Developed a centralized **Power BI reporting solution** integrating data from **Excel/CSV files and MySQL databases**, replacing legacy Excel-based reporting.
* Built an interactive **data model and business dashboards** to monitor revenue, expenses, sales, profitability, and departmental performance.
* Created and optimized **DAX measures** to support dynamic KPI tracking and business analysis.
* Used **DAX Studio** to optimize report performance, achieving a **5% improvement in report efficiency**.
* Analyzed **departmental revenue and expense trends** to identify improvement opportunities and support business targets of **10% revenue growth and 20% expense reduction**.
* Transformed complex business data into **actionable insights** to support management decision-making and performance monitoring.
